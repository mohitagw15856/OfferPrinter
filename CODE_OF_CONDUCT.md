# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a tool for people looking for work, which is a stressful and
often demoralising thing to be doing. Bring that awareness with you.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people using this tool.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Note that "roast mode" is a feature of the software, aimed at CV *writing*, and
is opt-in by the person whose CV it is. It is not a licence for how we talk to
each other.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers by opening a private security advisory or
contacting the repository owner directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of
any incident.
