# Senior Product Analyst — NorthBank (Remote, UK)

## About NorthBank
NorthBank is a fast-growing digital challenger bank on a mission to make everyday
banking effortless for over two million customers. Our Data team sits at the
heart of the product, shaping the decisions that define the customer experience.

## The role
We're looking for a Senior Product Analyst to partner with our Product and
Growth teams. You'll own the analytics for one or more product areas end to end —
from defining the metrics that matter, to running experiments, to telling the
story that moves the roadmap. You will also help level up the wider analytics
craft and mentor other analysts.

## What you'll do
- Own product analytics for a core area of the NorthBank app (e.g. onboarding,
  payments, or savings).
- Design, run and interpret A/B tests and experiments to guide product decisions.
- Build and maintain trusted, well-modelled datasets using SQL and dbt.
- Define and monitor the key product metrics and build dashboards the team relies on.
- Partner closely with Product Managers, Engineers and Designers.
- Mentor and coach junior analysts and raise the bar for analytical rigour.

## What we're looking for
- 4+ years in a data/product/growth analytics role.
- Strong SQL — you can model complex data confidently.
- Solid experience designing and analysing A/B tests / controlled experiments.
- Experience with dbt (or a strong desire to learn it quickly).
- Proficiency in Python or R for analysis.
- Experience defining product metrics and building dashboards.
- Excellent communication — you can turn analysis into a clear story for non-technical partners.
- Experience mentoring or leading other analysts.
- Financial services / fintech experience is a plus.

## Nice to have
- Familiarity with experimentation platforms (e.g. Statsig, Optimizely).
- Exposure to event-tracking tools such as Amplitude or Mixpanel.
