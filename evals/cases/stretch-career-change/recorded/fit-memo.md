# Fit Memo — Senior Data Engineer at Helix Logistics

## Snapshot
This is a long shot, and it is worth saying so plainly. The role asks for six
years of senior data engineering; the candidate has six years of teaching and a
2025 analytics certificate. The analytical instinct and the stakeholder work are
real and transferable, but the core engineering requirements are not met.

## Requirement-by-requirement
- **6+ years in data engineering, 2 at senior level** — Gap: the CV shows six
  years of teaching, with two years leading a department. No engineering roles.
- **Expert SQL and production Python** — Gap: the CV states "basic SQL" and
  "Python (pandas, beginner-intermediate)". That is not expert level.
- **dbt** — Gap: no evidence in the CV.
- **Snowflake** — Gap: no evidence in the CV.
- **Airflow** — Gap: no evidence in the CV.
- **Kafka** — Gap: no evidence in the CV.
- **AWS, Terraform, CI/CD** — Gap: no evidence in the CV.
- **Setting standards for a team** — Partial match: led a department of 4
  teachers and set schemes of work. Real leadership, different domain.
- **Turning analysis into decisions** — Strong match: analysed results across
  210 students and reorganised revision accordingly, with the pass rate rising
  from 71% to 82%.
- **Logistics domain** — Gap: no evidence in the CV.

## Honest gaps & how to talk about them
The engineering stack is missing entirely, and no amount of framing changes
that. If this role is the goal, the honest path is a junior or analyst-level
data role first, or a portfolio of real pipeline work. In conversation, lead
with the measurable analytical wins and the department leadership, and be
straightforward that the tooling is something being learned rather than owned.

## Bottom line
Not a credible application for this role as written. The transferable strengths
are genuine, but they answer a different job advert.
