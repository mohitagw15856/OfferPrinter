# Senior Data Engineer — Helix Logistics

Helix Logistics moves freight across Europe. We are hiring a Senior Data
Engineer to own our ingestion pipelines end to end.

## What you will do
- Design, build and operate production data pipelines processing ~40M events/day.
- Own our dbt models and the Snowflake warehouse they land in.
- Build streaming ingestion with Kafka and manage orchestration in Airflow.
- Deploy and monitor infrastructure on AWS using Terraform.
- Set engineering standards for a team of six data engineers.

## What we are looking for
- 6+ years in data engineering, at least 2 at senior level.
- Expert SQL and production Python. Strong software engineering fundamentals.
- Deep experience with dbt, Snowflake, Airflow and Kafka.
- Experience with AWS, Terraform, CI/CD and infrastructure as code.
- Logistics or supply-chain domain experience is a plus.
